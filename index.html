    How to play
    • Hands: pinch a knob or fader and move your hand to turn or slide it. Poke buttons with your fingertip. Touch the jog wheel and swirl it to nudge or scrub. Pinch the same knob twice quickly to reset it.
    • Controllers: point, hold the trigger, and move to turn or slide.
    • Desktop: click and drag the controls. The mouse wheel works on knobs and faders.
    • SYNC matches tempo and beat phase to the other deck. Moving the tempo fader turns sync off.
    • Add your MP3 or WAV files here before entering VR. Then pick them in the library panel on the left of the booth.
    • VENUE switches between passthrough and a virtual club. RECENTER moves the booth in front of you.
    • Scratching: in Turntable style, put your fingertip (or a pinch) on the record and move it back and forth. Let go and it plays on. In CDJ style, the top plate scratches and the outer ring bends the pitch. XF CUT gives the crossfader a sharp cut for scratch tricks.
    • TT / CDJ on the mixer switches the deck style.
    • NEXT SCENE changes the LED wall and laser pattern now. Scenes also change on their own on the timer you pick, at the start of a musical phrase.
    • WAVE cycles the waveform colors, which also recolor the visuals. VISUALS cycles Full, Chill and Off.
    • Tracks you add stay in this browser's library, so you only add them once per headset.
